Privacy Policy — SaibaTudo Química

Version 1.2 — 16 October 2026 (optional cloud AI; optional “Help improve the app”) · Applies to the website/PWA at saibatudo.net/quimica (and to the corresponding Android app, once published). This is a translation; the Portuguese page is the source text of the policy.

In one sentence: SaibaTudo Química has no sign-up, ads, trackers or usage analytics; in this version everything needed to look up elements and compounds and do calculations stays on your device, and nothing you type is sent to our servers, unless you tap “Ask the cloud AI” (or turn on automatic mode): then only the text of that question is sent, to be interpreted. If you turn on “Help improve the app” (off by default) or tap “Send this question”, the text of the questions the app did not understand is sent and stored for up to 4 months, without identifying you, to improve the interpreter.

1. Who we are

SaibaTudo is an independent, open-source (MIT licence), non-profit project. Responsible party: Francisco Aleixo (developer) — contact: saibatudo@saibatudo.net.

2. What we do NOT collect

We do not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, tax ID, contacts, photos or files. There is no login, no ads, no advertising SDKs, no Google Analytics, no Firebase Analytics and no third-party crash reporting. We do not sell or share data. The app does not use location, camera, microphone or contacts.

3. What stays only on your device

The questions you type and the calculations you make are processed on your device and are neither sent to nor stored by us. The browser keeps only: your theme, text size and language preferences (localStorage) and the app and data-package cache, so that it works offline. You can delete everything in Settings › Delete the data on this device, in your browser settings or by uninstalling the app. If you turn on “Help improve the app”, the queue of questions not yet sent (at most 30) is also kept in the preferences until it is sent or deleted.

5. Origin of the data shown

Open data with reuse licences: PubChem (NCBI/NLM), CODATA/NIST, Wikidata (CC0), Wikipedia and Wikibooks and the IUPAC Gold Book (CC BY-SA 4.0), ChEBI (CC BY 4.0), among others. The full list, with licences and dates, is in About the data. The values shown come from this data or from tested local calculations; no number is generated by an AI model.

6. Purpose and legal basis (LGPD, Brazilian Law 13.709/2018)

Fulfilling your query (art. 7, V) and keeping the service secure and free of abuse (art. 7, IX). Sending questions through “Help improve the app” depends on your consent (art. 7, I), which is separate from the cloud AI consent, off by default and can be withdrawn at any time in Settings. Apart from that, our servers process no personal data beyond the provider's technical logs.

7. Sharing and processors

Processors acting under our instructions: Vercel (hosting), GitHub (source code and automated publishing), Modal (running the cloud AI model, only for the questions you send), Upstash (the server’s Redis database: usage-limit counters and the questions sent through “Help improve the app”) and, in the future, Google Play (Android app distribution). These services may process data outside Brazil (for example, in the USA) and have their own policies.

8. Retention

We keep no user records or database. The hosting provider's technical logs (IP address, date, URL) follow the provider's own retention. Questions sent through “Help improve the app” are deleted automatically within 4 months (90 days after the end of the month they were sent); those a person reviews and approves may become, rewritten without any personal data, training examples for the interpreter.

9. Your rights

You may request confirmation, access, correction, anonymisation or deletion of the data we process, and withdraw consent (art. 18 of the LGPD), through the contact above. As we keep no records, there is usually no personal data to show.

10. Children

The app is educational and does not knowingly collect data from children. Chemical safety topics are informational: always read the product's safety data sheet and, with children, use under adult supervision.

11. Security

HTTPS connections, a restrictive content security policy (only the site's own resources) and digitally signed data packages: the app warns when a signature does not match. No system is 100% secure.

12. Changes

Relevant changes will be published on this page, with a new version date.
