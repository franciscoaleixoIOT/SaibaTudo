Privacy Policy — SaibaTudo LLMs

Version 1.0 — 25 October 2026 · Applies to the site/PWA at saibatudo.net/llms. The Portuguese page is the source text of this policy; this is a translation.

Other languages: Português (Brasil) · English · Español · Deutsch · Français · 中文

Source text (Portuguese)

In one sentence: SaibaTudo LLMs has no account, ads, trackers or usage analytics. The word vectors and every computation stay on your device, and nothing you type is sent to our servers unless you tap “Ask the cloud AI” (or turn on the automatic mode): then only that question, the language, a chart summary and lesson excerpts are sent, for the model to explain.

1. Who we are

SaibaTudo is an independent, open-source (MIT licence), non-profit project. Controller: Francisco Aleixo (developer) — contact: saibatudo@saibatudo.net.

2. What we do NOT collect

We do not ask for name, e-mail, phone, documents, contacts, photos or files. There is no login, no ads, no advertising SDKs, no Google Analytics, Firebase or third-party crash reporting. We do not sell or share data. The app does not use location, camera, microphone or contacts.

3. What stays only on your device

Browser storage holds: theme, text size, language, the history of your last 50 questions (only for the ↑/↓ keys of the field), the “automatic cloud AI” switch and, if you use the cloud AI, a random installation code (UUID), plus the cache of the app and of the vector package for offline use. “Delete the data on this device”, in Settings, removes all of it.

5. Origin of the data shown

The word vectors are GloVe 6B (Pennington, Socher & Manning, 2014; Wikipedia 2014 + Gigaword 5), published by Stanford University under the PDDL 1.0 licence: 100,000 English words, 50 dimensions, packaged and digitally signed by the project. The app verifies the signature before using the package. The lesson texts are written by the project.

6. Purpose and legal basis (LGPD, Brazilian Law 13.709/2018)

Loading connections are necessary to provide the service (art. 7, V). The cloud AI only works with your consent, by tap or by the switch in Settings (art. 7, I), and can be turned off at any time. No data is used for advertising or profiling.

7. Sharing and processors

Vercel (hosting of the site and of the function that forwards the question) and Hugging Face (server where the model runs, USA) act as processors, only to execute what you asked. “Report a problem” opens a public GitHub issue with the question in the title; nothing is sent until you confirm there.

8. Retention

We do not keep the question texts. The generated answer stays for up to 1 hour in an in-memory cache on the server, keyed by an irreversible digest (SHA-256 hash) of the question, language, context and snippets, without identification: identical questions get the same answer without calling the model again. Technical metrics (time, status, error) go to logs without the text. The installation code (UUID) travels with the question and serves, together with the IP address, only the usage limits (per minute and per day); the server keeps only a digest of it, never the code itself.

9. Your rights

Since almost nothing is collected, most rights are exercised on the device itself (delete data, turn off the cloud). For any request or question: saibatudo@saibatudo.net.

10. Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children and teenagers, and has no content aimed at children under 13. The cloud AI is optional and off by default.

11. Security

Everything travels over HTTPS. The vector package is signed (ECDSA P-256) and checked on the device; the site uses a strict content security policy (no external scripts) and loads nothing from third parties. The AI answer is verified on the server before it reaches you.

12. Changes

Changes to this policy are published here with a new version and date; the code of the app and of this page is public on GitHub, with history.
